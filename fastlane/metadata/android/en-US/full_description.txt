The JIIT webportal, as an app. Sign in once with your JIIT Google account and everything is a tap away, even offline.

JPortal is unofficial. It isn't made, affiliated with or endorsed by JIIT.

ATTENDANCE
• A ring for every subject, counted class by class
• How many classes you can skip, or how many you need to attend to get back to your target
• A calendar of every class, with the days you missed struck through
• Your lecture, tutorial and practical attendance separately

ACADEMICS
• SGPA and CGPA for every semester, with a what-if for the semesters to come
• Grade cards
• Marks for every exam event
• Your exam schedule with dates, rooms and seats, and a countdown to the next paper

SUBJECTS
• Every subject you've taken, searchable, with faculty and credits
• Your subject choices and MOOC requests

ME
• Profile, fees, hostel and bank details (hidden until you tap)
• Feedback forms: give one rating per teacher, review it, send

AND
• Works offline: everything is saved on your phone, and every screen says how old its data is
• Material You colours from your wallpaper, or the classic JPortal blue, with a pure black dark mode
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing about you ever reaches the developer

JPortal is open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/codelif/jportal-android
It's a remake of the original JPortal web app by Yash Malik.
